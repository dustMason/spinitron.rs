# Spinitron for iPhone

Jordan's personal iOS companion to the existing Mac menu-bar catalog browser.
Install locally through Xcode using a personal development team. iPhone, iOS
17+, portrait and landscape; no separate iPad layout or distribution service in
this release.

Browse a cached public catalog by station and Pacific broadcast date, search
show/artist/preview-song metadata, explicitly refresh, and tap a broadcast to
open its exact Spotify playlist. KALX is the default. No Spotify authentication,
library mutation, playback control, scraping, or background scheduling.

On October 13, 2026 Jordan explicitly selected **Silver Hi-Fi—the currently
installed Mac skin**, rather than the exploratory Radio Index concept. Preserve
that material and LCD language; adapt navigation, readable text and touch targets
to iPhone. Business logic and reusable visuals live in `../shared/`; Mac-specific
AppKit startup and window behavior remain in `../macos/`.
